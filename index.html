How ShieldSense Works

The 5-stage automated defense lifecycle starting from your Gmail account.

STAGE 01

1. Connect Gmail

Securely connect your Gmail account to open the ShieldSense Connected Dashboard.

STAGE 02

2. Inbox Analysis

ShieldSense reads incoming emails, extracting message headers, intent vectors, and metadata.

STAGE 03

3. Multi-Signal Investigation

Embedded links and file attachments are evaluated simultaneously as unified evidence.

STAGE 04

4. Risk Segregation

Emails are scored 0–100 and categorized into Safe (e.g. Safe (3)), Suspicious, and Dangerous.

STAGE 05

5. Action & Quarantine

Review transparent forensic evidence and isolate threats with non-destructive quarantine.
